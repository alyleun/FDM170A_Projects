Project 4: Conceptual Art

Original Wall Drawing From MASS MoCA: Wall Drawing 684A

Step By Step Procedure List

  1. First I will set up the color of the background as black. The background is basically a square-shaped area.
  2. Then I will draw four smaller square boxes which have the same size on the background.
  3. I will then draw the lines in the square boxes. There are four kinds of line which is vertical, horizontal, diagonal left and diagonal right.
  4. And finally I will fill in different colors between the lines.
  5. The vertical and horizontal lines are more easy to draw and coloring. But the diagonal lines are not good to manipulate. They even overthrow to each other.
  6. While I think I have finally solved all the problem I discover that I am not working on a square box but a rectangle! So that is the last thing I need to fix.
